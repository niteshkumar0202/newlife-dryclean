# NewLife 60-30-10 Theme

- 60% White / warm ivory: page backgrounds, content areas, major cards.
- 30% NewLife green: primary CTAs, brand emphasis, navigation accents, selected sections.
- 10% Muted gold: premium badges, highlights, borders and decorative accents.

Core palette:
- White: #FFFFFF
- Warm ivory: #FFFCF5
- NewLife green: #16835F
- Deep green: #173F35
- Soft green: #F3FAF6
- Muted gold: #C99A2E
- Soft gold: #FFF7DF
